UNAWATUNA BEACH



Overview

Unawatuna is one of the most popular beaches in Southern Sri Lanka, known for its crescent-shaped bay, calm shallow water, and lively beachfront strip of cafes and guesthouses.



Best for

Swimming, snorkeling, families, first-time visitors who want an easy, relaxed beach with lots of amenities nearby.



Activities

Swimming in calm, reef-protected water. Snorkeling near the rocky outcrops at the bay's edges. Short walk to Japanese Peace Pagoda for sunset views. Beachfront restaurants and bars for evening dining.



Best season

December to April (dry season, calm seas). Avoid May to September (monsoon, rougher water, some businesses close).



Crowd level

High, especially December to January. Expect a busier, more touristy atmosphere than quieter southern beaches like Talalla or Dickwella.



Nearest town

5 to 10 minutes from Galle Fort by tuk-tuk.



Safety notes

Generally safe for swimming due to the bay's natural protection, but always check local flag or warning signs before entering the water during monsoon season.
